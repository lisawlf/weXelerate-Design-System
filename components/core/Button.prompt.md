Ghost-pill CTA — transparent with a 1px ink border at rest, flooding to solid ink with white text on hover; use it for every action on a weXelerate surface.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary">Apply now</Button>
<Button variant="onDark" size="sm">Subscribe</Button>
<Button variant="brand" href="#programs">Explore programs</Button>
```

- `primary` on white/glass, `onDark` on the #0e0e0e band or over photography.
- `brand` is the filled teal button; its label is **always white**, never black.
- `spark` is lime with black text — at most one lime element per view, so use it for a single top CTA only.
- Sizes: sm 8/18, md 11/26 (default), lg 14/32. Pad to ≥44px height on touch layouts.
