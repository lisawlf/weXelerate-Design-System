Thin monochrome line icon that inherits `currentColor` — quiet and functional, never a decorative focal point.

```jsx
<Icon name="arrow-right" size={16} />
<Button icon={<Icon name="calendar" size={16} />}>Book a tour</Button>
```

- Never tint an icon with lime; ink on light surfaces, white on dark.
- Brand/social marks are the exception and use the platforms' own glyphs, monochrome.
