The liquid-glass slab every weXelerate box inherits — translucent, blurred, saturated, with a refracted rim and a diagonal glint.

```jsx
<GlassPanel density="chrome" radius="sm" padding="8px">…nav row…</GlassPanel>
<GlassPanel tone="dark">…on the #0e0e0e footer band…</GlassPanel>
```

- Hairlines divide *sections*; glass builds *boxes*. Never replace a hairline with glass.
- Never tint glass with teal, navy or lime, and never nest glass inside glass.
- The opaque fallback ships automatically via `@supports` / `prefers-reduced-transparency`.
