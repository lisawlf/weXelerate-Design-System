What a weXelerate partnership actually involves

We run applied innovation programs for corporates in the DACH region: we scope the problem with your team, source the startups, run the pilot, and hand the working result back to the people who will own it.

How the engagement runs

Four stages over roughly five months. Scoping fixes the problem statement and the internal owner. Sourcing produces a shortlist from our ecosystem and outside it. The pilot runs eight to twelve weeks against a metric agreed up front. Handover moves the result and the vendor relationship to your team.

What we need from you

One named owner with budget authority, two hours a fortnight from a technical contact, and a decision at the end of the pilot. Programs that fail almost always fail on the first of those.

Next step

Book a 90-minute tour of the building. You meet three teams working on something adjacent to your problem — no deck, no pitch.
